# ExamSathi Nepal — Standard MCQ Markdown Ingestion Format

Use this format to write and upload multiple-choice questions (MCQs) in bulk for any chapter or assessment.
Each question must contain:

1. A question header (starting with `### Question` or `###`)
2. Exactly 4 options (`- A)`, `- B)`, `- C)`, `- D)`)
3. A correct answer indicator (`Answer: B`)
4. (Optional) Detailed explanation (`Explanation: ...`)
5. (Optional) Difficulty level (`Difficulty: EASY | MEDIUM | HARD`)
6. (Optional) Marks and negative penalty (`Marks: 1.0`, `Negative: 0.2`)

### Question 1: What is the primary role of adding Gypsum ($CaSO_4 \cdot 2H_2O$) to cement clinker during grinding?

- A) To accelerate the early strength development of concrete
- B) To retard the initial setting time and prevent flash set
- C) To enhance resistance to sulfate attack in marine structures
- D) To reduce the total heat of hydration generated by $C_3A$
  Answer: B
  Explanation: Gypsum is added (around 2% to 3%) during the grinding of clinker to retard the rapid hydration of Tricalcium Aluminate (C3A), thereby preventing immediate flash setting of Ordinary Portland Cement.
  Difficulty: MEDIUM
  Marks: 1.0
  Negative: 0.2

### Question 2: Which Bogue compound in Portland cement is primarily responsible for early strength development within the first 7 days?

- A) Dicalcium Silicate ($C_2S$)
- B) Tricalcium Silicate ($C_3S$)
- C) Tricalcium Aluminate ($C_3A$)
- D) Tetracalcium Aluminoferrite ($C_4AF$)
  Answer: B
  Explanation: Tricalcium Silicate (C3S) hydrates rapidly and develops early compressive strength within 7 days, whereas Dicalcium Silicate (C2S) contributes to long-term strength beyond 28 days.
  Difficulty: EASY
  Marks: 1.0
  Negative: 0.2

### Question 3: According to Nepal Standard (NS 40) / IS 4031, what is the minimum initial setting time specified for Ordinary Portland Cement (OPC)?

- A) 15 minutes
- B) 30 minutes
- C) 45 minutes
- D) 60 minutes
  Answer: B
  Explanation: As per NS 40 and IS 4031 standards, the initial setting time of Ordinary Portland Cement determined via Vicat needle must not be less than 30 minutes.
  Difficulty: EASY
  Marks: 1.0
  Negative: 0.2

- A) 1.0
- B) 1.25
- C) 1.5

  Answer: B
